# FreshMart Business Performance Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

FreshMart Business Performance Analysis is an Excel-based data analytics project developed to evaluate sales performance, revenue, profitability, product categories, regional performance, customer membership, sales channels, discounts, and product returns.

The project uses Microsoft Excel for data profiling, data cleaning, exploratory analysis, KPI development, Pivot Table analysis, and dashboard creation to transform raw transaction data into meaningful business insights.

## 2. Business Understanding

FreshMart operates across different product categories, regions, store types, customer membership levels, and sales channels.

The business generates transaction-level data containing information about customers, products, stores, sales, discounts, revenue, profit, membership, sales channels, and transaction dates.

Analyzing this information helps evaluate overall business performance, identify high-performing areas, and recognize opportunities for improvement.

## 3. Business Questions

The analysis was designed to answer the following key business questions:

1. What are the monthly revenue and profit trends?
2. Which product categories perform best in terms of revenue and profit?
3. Which regions and stores contribute the highest sales?
4. Which customer membership levels generate the highest revenue?
5. How does revenue vary by sales channel and day type?
6. How do discounts affect profit margin?
7. What is the product return rate?

## 4. Dataset Overview

The project uses multiple datasets related to FreshMart's business operations.

| Dataset | Records | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Sales Transactions | 2,500 | Contains transaction-level sales, quantity, discount, revenue, cost, profit, payment mode, sales channel, return status, and day type information |
| Customers | 700 | Contains customer and membership information |
| Products | 150 | Contains product, category, subcategory, brand, cost, selling price, and season information |
| Stores | 180 | Contains store, region, store type, and monthly target information |
| Employees | 350 | Contains employee and department information |
| Suppliers | 85 | Contains supplier-related information |

The transaction data covers the period from March 2025 to March 2026.

## 5. Data Profiling

Data profiling was performed to understand the structure, completeness, consistency, and quality of the available datasets.

The data was reviewed for missing or blank values, unknown entries, inconsistent categorical values, duplicate records, and other data-quality issues before analysis.

## 6. Data Cleaning

The identified data-quality issues were reviewed and corrected before performing the analysis.

The cleaning process included handling blank and unknown values, standardizing inconsistent entries, checking duplicate records, and organizing the datasets for analysis.

The cleaned data was validated to ensure consistency and reliability for further analysis.

## 7. Data Analysis

Exploratory data analysis was performed using Microsoft Excel to evaluate FreshMart's business performance and identify important trends and patterns.

The analysis focused on:

- Monthly Sales and Profit Trends
- Regional Sales Performance
- Top 10 Stores by Sales
- Product Category Performance
- Sales Channel Comparison
- Membership Segment Analysis
- Discount Band vs Profit Margin
- Return Rate Overview

Pivot Tables, PivotCharts, Excel formulas, slicers, and comparisons were used to summarize the data and identify meaningful business patterns.

## 9. Dashboard Development

An interactive Excel dashboard was developed to provide a consolidated view of FreshMart's business performance.

The dashboard presents key performance indicators and visual analysis covering monthly sales and profit trend, sales by region, top 10 stores, product category performance, sales channel comparison, membership segment analysis, discount band vs profit margin, and return rate overview.

Slicers for Category, Region, Sales Channel, Membership, Return Status, and Store Type, along with a Transaction Date timeline, allow all charts to be filtered together.

## 10. Key Insights

- October 2025 recorded the highest monthly revenue and profit.
- Beverages generated the highest revenue, while Bakery generated the highest profit and the best profit margin (about 41%).
- The South region contributed about 65% of total sales, while the East region contributed only about 2%.
- Gold members generated the highest membership revenue, about 60% of total sales.
- In-Store generated the highest sales revenue (about 69%), followed by Online (about 21%) and Mobile App (about 10%).
- Online and Mobile App sales were slightly higher on weekends.
- Weekend sales were similar to weekday sales, but weekend profit was much higher.
- Profit margin falls from about 31% with no discount to about 14% at a 20% discount.
- About 4.6% of orders (114 of 2,500) were returned.

## 11. Recommendations

- Focus on high-performing products while improving low-margin categories such as Frozen Foods and Oil.
- Strengthen sales strategies in lower-performing regions, especially East and North.
- Retain and engage high-value Gold members through targeted offers and loyalty strategies.
- Review deeper discount levels (15% to 20%), as they reduce profit margin without a matching increase in sales.
- Increase promotional activities across Online and Mobile App sales channels.
- Investigate the main reasons for product returns to reduce the return rate.
- Monitor monthly revenue and profit trends to support better business planning.
